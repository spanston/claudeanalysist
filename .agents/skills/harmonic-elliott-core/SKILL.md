# Harmonic Elliott Wave — Core Structure & Ratio System

Source: Ian Copsey, *Fractal Forecasting* (+ CastAwayTrader HEW course). HEW replaces
classical Elliott's variable structure with ONE consistent fractal and a quantified
ratio system. Structure is subservient to ratios: ugly counts with valid ratios beat
beautiful counts that miss ratio requirements.

## 1. The five-wave fractal

A five-wave move = (i)(ii)(iii)(iv)(v) where:

- **(i), (iii), (v) are each constructed of (a)(b)(c)** — three subwaves, not five.
- **Only Wave (a) and Wave (c) may internally be five-wave.** Wave (b) is always corrective.
- Recursive: each (a) and (c) is itself a five-wave fractal one degree lower.
- A 5-wave fractal at one degree = an (a) or (c) leg of the next higher degree.

Eliminated from classical Elliott: extended waves, failed fifths, leading/ending
diagonals, running corrections, "massive expanded flats" — all absorbed into the
standard structure. One structure, no exceptions.

Degree ladder: a-b-c/i-v → (a)-(c)/(i)-(v) → A-C/1-5 → (A)-(C)/(1)-(5) → [A]-[C]/[1]-[5].

## 2. The six hard rules (any violation → discard the count)

1. Wave (ii) never moves beyond the start of Wave (i).
2. Wave (iii) always travels beyond the extreme of Wave (i).
3. Wave (iii) is never the shortest of (i),(iii),(v); it must be longer than (i).
4. **Wave (iv) must not breach — and normally not even reach — the extreme of Wave (b) of (iii).**
   (This REPLACES the classical wave-1/wave-4 overlap rule; overlap of (i) and (iv) is allowed in principle but nearly vacuous given rule 6.)
5. Wave (v) normally exceeds the extreme of Wave (iii) ("normally", not always).
6. **Wave (iii) ≥ 176.4% × Wave (i)** (projected from the (ii) extreme). Rare tolerance: 172–176.4%.

Supporting rules:
- Inside Wave (iii): **Wave (c) of (iii) ≥ Wave (a) of (iii)** (rare tolerated exception in Wave (i): c ≥ 85.4% of a).
- In any A-B-C: B must not breach the start of A; C should exceed (or at least retest) A's extreme.
- Inside every (a) and (c): internal 5-wave count with valid ratios AND valid internal alternation — otherwise the count is wrong even if the big ratios look right.

## 3. Projection ratio tables

### Wave (iii) = |Wave (i)| × ratio, projected from the (ii) extreme
- **Minimum: 176.4%** (hard, rule 6).
- Clusters: **176.4–198.7 | 223.6, 238.2, 261.8–298.7 | 323.6, 338.2, 361.8–398.7 | 423.6…**
  General form: X23.6, X38.2, X61.8–X98.7, X incrementing by 100.
- Common singles: 176.4, 185.4, 190.2, 198.7, 223.6, 238.2, 261.8, 276.4, 285.4, 298.7.

### Wave (c) = |Wave (a)| × ratio, projected from the (b) extreme
- Below 100%: 61.8, 66.7, 76.4, 85.4, 91, 94.4, 98.7
- Above 100%: 102.1, 105.6, 109(.2), 114.6, 123.6, 138.2, 141.4, 150, 158.6, 161.8, 176.4, 185.4, 200, 223.6, 261.8
- Most common: 109.2, 114.6, 123.6. Equality (100%) common.
- **(c) projections are subservient to (iii) projections** — when they conflict, trust (iii).

### Wave (v) = |(i)→(iii)| × ratio, projected from the (iv) extreme
- Set: 23.6, 30, 33.3, 38.2, 41.4, 50, 58.6, 61.8, 66.7, 76.4, 85.4
- **Average ≈ 50%; minimum ≈ 23.6%; normal maximum 76.4%.** May stall between ratios — treat as a distribution, not a rule.
- Same-degree reuse: (v) ≈ 223.6% × (i) also observed.

### Retracement set — valid for (ii), (iv), (b), (x)
9.0, 14.6, 23.6, 33.3, 38.2, 41.4, 50, 58.6, 61.8, 66.7, 76.4, 85.4, 90.2, 94.4, 95.4, 96.6, 97.9, 98.7, 100 (%)
- √2-derived 41.4/58.6 appear most often in corrections.
- Wave-2 typical: deep allowed (14.6–100%); Wave-4 typical: 23.6–66.7.
- Complement pairs: 38.2/61.8, 23.6/76.4, 14.6/85.4, 9.0/91.0.

### Positional caps
- Wave (b) of Wave (iii): max 76.4–85.4% retrace, occasionally 90%.
- Wave (b) of (i) or (v): up to 100%.
- Wave (b) otherwise: the wild card — 5.6–100%, no standard ratios.

## 4. Numeric alternation (guideline, ~90–95% reliable)

**(ii)% + (iv)% ≈ 80–100% in trending moves; 100–120% in corrective structures** (US equities often 100–110%+).
Alternation applies to both depth AND complexity (deep+complex vs shallow+simple).

Exceptions (compensation mechanisms):
- (ii) > 90% → (iv) may be 14.6–23.6% (up to 50% in corrections).
- Deep (b) of (iii) (>66.7% to 85.4%) compensates → (iv) shallower, sum < 80%.
- Sum < ~75% → expect a very deep (b) of (v).

**Alternation failure as signal**: if a "(iii) target" is hit precisely but (iv) falls short of required depth, the wave was actually iii of (c) of (iii) — promote the target to the next (iii) cluster.

## 5. Triple coincidence (core validation)

At every degree, three projections must point to the same target:

**target[(iii)] = target[(c) of (iii)] = target[v of (c) of (iii)]**

If the (c)-of-(iii) target cannot match a (iii) cluster target, the count is wrong
(or it is a triple three, i.e. corrective). Stack ratio grids of adjacent degrees and
trade only cluster intersections.

## 6. Aftermath targets (post-completion)

- After a completed Wave (v): first reversal → **span of Wave (b) of (v)**; first or second reversal → **prior Wave (iv)** / prior swing. First reversal after a fifth often develops quickly.
- After a corrective Wave C completes: price retests the **prior Wave B** area.
- Zigzag danger: a "disproportionately big, almost parabolic" C with no pullbacks is often NOT corrective — do not trust it.

## 7. What HEW does NOT give you

- No volume model (unlike classical EW lore). Confirmation is momentum-only (see harmonic-elliott-process).
- Wave (v) is a distribution, not fixed ratios.
- Wave (b) depth is unknowable in advance; Wave (ii) depth unknowable (14.6–100%).
- Triangle mid-leg ratios are OCR-ambiguous in the source — treat 66.7–76.4% as approximate (see harmonic-elliott-corrections).
