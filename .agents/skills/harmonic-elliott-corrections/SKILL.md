# Harmonic Elliott Wave — Corrective Structures

Source: Ian Copsey, *Fractal Forecasting* + corrective-structures course material.
Corrections occur **only** in Wave (ii), (iv), (b), (x). Any corrective structure may
appear in any position **except: Wave (ii) is never a triangle**. Triangles appear only
in (b), (x), (iv). Corrections nest fractally. In corrective (vs trending) sequences,
ratios stretch toward the limits of their ranges.

## 1. Classification

- **Correction through price** (deep): simple zigzag — retraces ≥61.8% of the preceding move.
- **Correction through time** (shallow/sideways): flats, triangles, double/triple threes.
- Any three-wave move is weaker than a five-wave move of the same degree.

## 2. Zigzag — 5-3-5

- (a) and (c) are each internally five-wave; (b) is any corrective structure.
- Geometry: (b) must stay beyond the start of (a); (c) must exceed the (a) extreme.
- (c) ≈ (a) in size is typical; engine ratios: (c) = 61.8–185.4% × (a), most common 100/109.2/114.6/123.6.
- Double zigzag: (a)(b)(c)-(x)-(a)(b)(c). (x) any corrective; normally does not breach (b), but allow deeper.
- Triple three: three zigzags + two (x). **Discriminant vs impulse: triple threes fail the impulsive projection requirements and alternation** — if the balance of legs fails ratio/alternation checks, it is corrective. Second (x) does not penetrate first (x).

## 3. Flat family — 3-3-5

First leg is always 3-wave. Discriminator matrix (b-end vs start-of-a; c-end vs a-extreme):

| Variant   | Wave B vs start of A        | Wave C vs extreme of A          |
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Regular   | retests (≈ double top)      | holds near (≈ double bottom)     |
| Expanded  | exceeds (new extreme)       | exceeds (new extreme other side) |
| Running   | exceeds (new extreme)       | fails to reach                   |

- Regular flat: FC = 5 waves back to ≈ FA, normally just beyond, occasionally falls just short. FC rarely extends much beyond FA.
- **Expanded flat: EFB exceeds the prior extreme by 14.6–38.2% of EFA's depth** (occasionally up to 50% in equities/illiquid markets). EFC = 5 waves, normally just beyond EFA's end. EFB can be any corrective combination.
- No exact C-target ratio: "normally just beyond A" — deliberately fuzzy. Use retracement confluence instead.

## 4. Triangle — a-b-c-d-e (each leg 3-wave: 3-3-3-3-3)

- Legs contract: each leg from b onward is shorter than the previous; a longest, e shortest.
- Leg ratios (approximate): **/\B = 76.4–85.4% of /\A; /\C = 66.7–76.4% of /\B; /\D = 66.7–76.4% of /\C; /\E = 50–66.7% of /\D.** Occasionally all legs up to 85.4%.
- Irregular variant: one leg expands beyond the prior extreme, max ≈38.2%; the leg after the expansion runs 85.4–105.6%.
- /\A must be 3-wave; **/\E must always be 3-wave** and not too close to the apex (unstable).
- Location: (iv), (b), (x) only — **never (ii)**. In an uptrend it never makes a higher high.
- Wave-e quirks: may fake a breakdown (stop-run then sharp reversal) or truncate — but a truncated e must still complete its internal a-b-c.
- Price enters and leaves a triangle in the same direction: post-triangle, trend resumes with a spiky move.
- Volume (only volume note in the sources): size and volume decrease across legs 4–5.

## 5. Complex corrections — W-X-Y(-XX-Z)

- Double three: two different simple patterns joined by X. Triple three: three patterns, two connectors.
- X-wave character: ugly, technical, often no countable subdivision — its only job is connecting two corrective patterns.
- **Projection: Y = 100% × W, projected from the end of X.** Y may not make a much lower low than W — it burns time.
- Common locations: after a strong rally (wave 4 after wave 3), and wave B.
- Completion test: was the correction deep/long enough to alter sentiment? If the move off W-X-Y is weak and cannot make a new extreme (too shallow, quick, unscary), expect extension into a triple three.

## 6. Depth specifications per position

| Position | Depth spec |
|---|---|
| Wave (ii) | 14.6–100%; never a triangle; unknowable in advance |
| Wave (iv) | By alternation vs (ii) (sum rule); must not reach (b) of (iii) |
| Wave (b) of (iii) | ≤76.4–85.4% (occasionally 90%) |
| Wave (b) of (i)/(v) | up to 100% |
| Wave (b) elsewhere | wild card 5.6–100% |
| Wave (x) | 5.6% min, rarely <14.6%; normally ≤ prior (b); 2nd (x) ≤ 1st (x) |

## 7. Critical supports (invalidation anchors)

- Critical support for Wave 2 = start of Wave 1.
- Critical support for Wave 4 = low of **B of 3** (counter-trend B waves respect this and the major retracement fibs).
- Critical support for Wave B = start of Wave A.
- Supports ratchet: start of (i) → low of (ii) → low of (b) of (iii) → low of (iv) → low of (b) of (v).

## 8. Completion & invalidation heuristics

- No sign tells you in advance whether the first a-b-c is the whole correction or extends.
- A single zigzag down does NOT confirm correction completion (could become double/flat).
  Confirmation of trend resumption: price breaks the 38.2–50% expansion zone of a potential expanded flat.
- Practical bottoming: (i) micro-count completion of the corrective structure + (ii) major retracement levels of the preceding impulse — trade the confluence.
- Progress marker of a working correction: each new a-b-c leg makes lower lows and lower highs, cooling sentiment.
- Failed-completion signature: weak bounce off an apparent W-X-Y that cannot make a new extreme → expect extension.
