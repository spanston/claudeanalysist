# Harmonic Elliott Wave — Counting & Forecasting Process

Source: Ian Copsey, *Fractal Forecasting* + HEW course. The process is deliberately
**top-to-down**: zoom out, recognize markers, then verify against the six rules.
Never count micro structure without the bigger picture. Ratios over structural beauty.

## 1. Top-to-down marker process (five waves up)

Setup: weekly or at least daily chart. Scan for three visual markers:

1. **Marker 1 — candidate Wave 3**: the longest a-b-c move, located in the middle of the trending structure.
2. **Marker 2 — Waves 4+5**: a corrective a-b-c against the trend followed by an a-b-c that makes a NEW extreme; must come after Marker 1.
3. **Marker 3 — Waves 1+2**: the starting a-b-c off the significant low followed by a corrective a-b-c making a higher low; must come before Marker 1.

Verification (fail any → back to marker search):
- **Step 2**: project 176.4% × Wave 1 from the Wave-2 extreme — Wave 3 must reach at least that.
- **Step 3**: Wave 5 must make a new extreme over Wave 3 AND be subdivisible into a-b-c.
- **Step 4 — critical-support cross-checks** (all four): low of 2 ≥ start of 1; low of B of 3 ≥ low of 2; low of 4 ≥ low of B of 3; low of B of 5 ≥ low of 4.
- **Step 5**: inside Wave 3, C of 3 ≥ A of 3.
- Optional: micro-count A and C of 1/3/5 as five smaller waves.

Mirror for down moves: find a-b-c wave 1 down; wave 2 up must hold under wave 1's start (that is the invalidation); then wave b of 3 must not violate the top of wave 2; c ≥ a; candidate wave 3 ≥ 176.4% × wave 1. Rule of thumb: don't catch the falling knife — wait for the golden (1)-(2)-1-2 setup.

## 2. Counting a chart from scratch (7 steps)

1. Weekly: locate major low/top, macro trend direction.
2. Daily: identify a-b-c wave 1 + corrective a-b-c wave 2 making a higher low.
3. Look for wave A of 3.
4. After shallow B of 3, expect the strongest rally — C of 3.
5. Top of C of 3 → corrective wave 4.
6. Best confirmation of wave 5: wave A of 5 retests the top of C of 3 but fails to sustain the breakout.
7. Only then drop to lower timeframes for wave B of 5 micro structure.

Carry 2+ alternative micro counts aligned with the macro count; collect evidence or wait.

## 3. Forecast construction — target clustering

1. **Triple coincidence at every degree**: target[(iii)] = target[(c) of (iii)] = target[v of (c) of (iii)]. Stack the ratio grids of adjacent degrees; keep only intersections.
2. **Cluster zones from paired ratios** — worked example (DOW): 176.4%(iii) ∧ 85.4%(c) → zone 1; 190.2%(iii) ∧ 95.4%(c) → zone 2; 198.7%(iii) ∧ 100%(c) → zone 3 ⇒ general target area = zone-1 low to zone-3 high.
3. **Multi-degree narrowing**: higher degrees give wide zones; each lower degree narrows. (USDJPY example: monthly range → hourly fractal narrowed the final low to a 0.7% band; actual landed inside.)
4. **Wave (iv) pre-computation**: from (ii) depth + the alternation sum, compute required (iv) depth; reject any (iii) projection whose implied (iv) would breach (b) of (iii).
5. **Iterative promotion**: as internal legs complete, re-match; if -c- of -iii- overshoots the ratio needed for the current (iii) candidate, promote the target to the next cluster (223.6/238.2/…).
6. Context targets: Wave (A) commonly stalls just below/above the prior Wave ((III)) extreme; corrective tops want confluence of a projection fib AND a retracement fib.
7. Tolerances: (v) may be imperfect (47.5% vs 50%) if the higher-degree target is valid; rare (iii) anomalies down to ~172%. A single projection hit is "good but not great" — demand dense clusters.

## 4. Invalidation discipline

- Critical supports/resistances are THE invalidation levels and they ratchet (see harmonic-elliott-corrections §7). Move the level only when the next checkpoint confirms.
- "As soon as any of the six rules of the five-wave fractal is violated, discard the count. If you have no other count, close the position."
- Warning: after a rule break the market may keep pretending the count is alive — the damaged structure eventually breaks apart and hurts those who don't follow hard rules.
- At critical junctures with conflicting scenarios, act only when two — better three — alternative scenarios point the same way.

## 5. Momentum confirmation (no volume model in HEW)

- At a projected (iii)/(v)/(c) target, require momentum confirmation of reversal. If indicators say the trend is intact, the count is wrong.
- Divergence (price new extreme, momentum not) is expected as (iii)/(v)/(c) targets are approached; near-certain at v-of-(c) turns. **Divergence is not a signal by itself — confirm with a break of the most recent swing or a price-reversal pattern.**
- **Double divergence** (two successive momentum peaks/troughs against price) = high-probability larger reversal.
- Multi-timeframe override: if two higher-timeframe momentum gauges both push further into overbought/oversold together, trend strength overrides a pending count/divergence.
- RSI overbought/oversold signals are valid only when NOT trending.
- Key reversal bars (day/week/month) confirm major wave completions.
- If indicators conflict with the count: reduce/exit, then overhaul the count.

## 6. Practical pitfalls

- Zigzag-indicator trap: major swing highs/lows are not necessarily wave boundaries.
- Sharp news-driven moves: wait for the first correction deep enough to satisfy alternation before labeling.
- Daily bars hide lower-degree structure; drop timeframes when opaque.
- A count that fits ratios but whose Wave v is internally 5-wave (not 3-wave) is invalid.
- Triangles are flighty: legs can whip past prior extremes before reversing.
- Wave (b) absorbs shocks — unforecastable in depth. Wave (ii) depth unknowable.
- A single projection ratio hit is not a target — only clusters are.
